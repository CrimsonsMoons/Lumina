Blackjack · Table rules

  1. Goal: Beat the dealer by reaching 21 or closer without busting. Aces are 1 or 11.
  2. Betting: Play 1–3 starting hands, each with the same opening bet. There is no fixed 250,000-chip table limit; you can wager up to the virtual chips you own. Enter an exact amount using Set amount, or add more with + Add. Custom wagers support two decimal places, with a minimum opening wager of ◈ 5 per hand. All In automatically uses your full virtual bankroll for one hand and clears side wagers. All chips are fictional, not real money.
  3. Natural Blackjack: Original two-card Ace + 10-value hand pays 3:2. A 21 after splitting is an ordinary 21, not a natural.
  4. Hit, Stand, Double: Double your bet on a two-card hand and take exactly one card. Double after split is allowed if you have chips.
  5. Split: Split identical ranks, with enough virtual chips to match the bet, up to four total hands. Split Aces receive one card each and cannot be re-split.
  6. Surrender: Late surrender is offered before acting on an original two-card hand, after the dealer checks for Blackjack. You lose half that hand’s bet.
  7. Insurance: Offered only when the dealer shows an Ace, costs half the main wager total, pays 2:1 profit on dealer natural Blackjack, otherwise loses.
  8. Dealer: Six decks, shuffled near a cut card; the dealer checks Blackjack before player decisions. Default: stands on all 17. Optional: hits soft 17.
  9. Perfect Pairs: First hand’s two cards: mixed pair 5:1; same-color pair 12:1; same-suit pair 25:1.
  10. 21+3: First hand’s opening cards plus dealer upcard: flush 5:1; straight 10:1; trips 30:1; straight flush 40:1; suited trips 100:1. Side bets are settled separately from main wagers.
  11. Add money: Use the green Add money to your balance panel to credit any custom amount of virtual chips directly to your wallet. This does not place a bet. No real-money payments are possible.
  12. Out of chips? When you have fewer than 5 chips left and no outstanding wager, you can optionally work a three-stage manual-labor shift. Scrub tables, drag trash bags, then mop. A finished shift pays ◈ 35 virtual chips. Quit cleaning using the X or Quit button; unfinished work is saved and can be resumed later.
